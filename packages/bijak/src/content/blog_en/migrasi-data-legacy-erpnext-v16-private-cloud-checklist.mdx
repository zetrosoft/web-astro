### Data Audit & Cleansing Prior to Migration

The fundamental law of data migration remains unchanged: *Garbage In, Garbage Out*. Importing dirty data into Frappe Framework v16 only accelerates operational chaos.

A thorough pre-migration data audit must address four core operational areas:
1. **Master Data Sanitization:** Deduplicate Customer, Supplier, and Item Master records. Standardize SKU structures and naming rules.
2. **Open Transaction Resolution:** Complete open Purchase Orders (PO), unfulfilled Sales Orders (SO), and clear old outstanding invoices.
3. **Financial Reconciliation:** Verify that General Ledger, Accounts Payable (AP), and Accounts Receivable (AR) balances match audited financial statements down to the cent.
4. **Data Normalization:** Align Units of Measure (UOM), currency tables, and Cost Center structures.

Before launching data extract scripts, leadership must choose a migration path that aligns with acceptable operational downtime limits.

#### Table 1: Comparison of Three Data Migration Strategies for ERPNext v16

| Migration Strategy | Operational Model | Advantages | Disadvantages | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Big Bang Migration** | Complete cut-over and legacy system shutdown executed over a single weekend. | Lower implementation costs, no double data entry, instant transition. | High operational risk if validation fails; demands exhaustive pre-testing. | Mid-market enterprises, SMEs, or businesses with isolated daily transactions. |
| **Phased Migration** | Rollout executed by module (e.g., Finance & Purchasing first, then Manufacturing & POS) or business unit. | Risk isolated per phase, easier user adoption, focused bug fixing. | Requires temporary integration bridges between legacy ERP and ERPNext v16; longer project timeline. | Multi-entity conglomerates, complex manufacturers, or multi-branch operations. |
| **Parallel Running** | Legacy system and ERPNext v16 run concurrently for 1–3 months until stability is verified. | Highest safety net, instant rollback capability in case of operational issues. | Dual operational costs, high risk of employee burnout due to double entry. | Banking, financial institutions, or highly regulated enterprise environments. |

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. **How long does a legacy ERP migration to ERPNext v16 usually take?**
   For a mid-sized enterprise managing up to 100,000 master records, auditing, schema mapping, staging, and cut-over typically require 4 to 8 weeks depending on legacy data quality.

2. **Should we import 5 years of historical transaction logs into ERPNext v16?**
   No. Standard practice is importing Opening Balances and active open transactions. Keep historical data in a read-only archive to maintain peak performance on the new database.

3. **Why choose private cloud hosting over multi-tenant SaaS for ERPNext v16?**
   Private cloud delivers complete data sovereignty, unrestricted schema customization, and predictable infrastructure costs that do not scale up with headcount.
