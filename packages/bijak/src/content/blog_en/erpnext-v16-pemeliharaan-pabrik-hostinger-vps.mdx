### Strategies for Automated Preventive Maintenance and Work Orders

Moving from reactive breakdown maintenance to structured preventive maintenance requires automated work instructions and scheduling discipline. In ERPNext v16, every piece of machinery is registered as an Item and Asset DocType, complete with digital records including serial numbers, acquisition dates, bay locations, and warranty details.

Through the Maintenance Schedule feature, engineering teams can configure inspection calendars based on time intervals (daily, weekly, monthly) or meter-based usage metrics (such as machine operational hours). When an inspection threshold is reached, the system automatically generates a Maintenance Work Order assigned to designated technicians. Technicians can access work orders via mobile devices, record findings, attach component photographs, and log spare parts consumption in real time.

If an anomaly requires emergency repair, the system links the Maintenance Visit to a corrective Work Order, automatically deducting used spare parts from technical inventory. If critical spare parts drop below pre-defined safety thresholds, the purchasing module initiates automated material requests.

### Downtime Transparency and Critical Spare Parts Optimization

Accurate downtime logging is essential for evaluating Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE). In ERPNext v16, every breakdown incident is logged alongside root cause categories—distinguishing between mechanical failure, electrical issues, or operator error.

This transactional data feeds interactive analytical dashboards that display:
- **Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF):** Measures the average operational uptime between equipment failures.
- **Mean Time to Repair (MTTR):** Evaluates technician repair response and resolution efficiency.
- **Asset Depreciation & Maintenance Cost Ratio:** Highlights machinery where cumulative maintenance costs exceed economic value, supporting timely replacement decisions.

These metrics empower executive leadership to make evidence-based capital expenditure (CAPEX) decisions rather than relying on guesswork.
