### Declarative Configuration: Docker Compose & Traefik v3 Labels

To ensure maximum throughput and reproducible environments, services are defined declaratively. Below is an essential declarative micro-snippet featuring Docker Compose labels for Traefik v3 dynamic routing and Valkey resource limits:

```yaml
version: '3.8'
services:
  valkey-cache:
    image: valkey/valkey:7.2-alpine
    command: valkey-server --maxmemory 512mb --maxmemory-policy allkeys-lru
    deploy:
      resources:
        limits:
          cpus: '1.0'
          memory: 512M
    networks:
      - internal-net

  api-service:
    image: my-app/api:v1.2
    deploy:
      resources:
        limits:
          cpus: '1.5'
          memory: 1024M
    labels:
      - "traefik.enable=true"
      - "traefik.http.routers.api.rule=Host(`api.yourdomain.com`)"
      - "traefik.http.routers.api.entrypoints=websecure"
      - "traefik.http.routers.api.tls.certresolver=letsencrypt"
    networks:
      - internal-net
      - coolify-proxy
```

When scaling distributed caching layers to sustain heavy concurrent throughput, referencing [Global Distributed Cache Design for Massive Scale](/en/blog/arsitektur-cache-terdistribusi-global-aplikasi-skala-raksasa/) becomes essential to prevent I/O bottlenecks across your database backend.
