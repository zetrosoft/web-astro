### FEFO Accuracy at Multi-Branch Scale

For regional distributors, the biggest challenge is ensuring items with the nearest expiration dates are picked first. A FEFO (First-Expired-First-Out) strategy requires the system to automatically suggest items based on batch numbers, regardless of their physical shelf location.

Data synchronization between branches often lags due to poor system architecture. This is where the [ERPNext v16 architecture for distribution companies](/en/blog/arsitektur-erpnext-v16-distribusi-manufaktur-vps-kvm/) proves its worth. Built on the Frappe Framework, it consolidates data from hundreds of distribution points into a single source of truth without performance bottlenecks.
