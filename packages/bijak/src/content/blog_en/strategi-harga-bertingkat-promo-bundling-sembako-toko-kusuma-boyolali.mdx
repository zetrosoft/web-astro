### Common Challenges for Warung Entrepreneurs

Warungs, as the frontline of essential goods distribution, are often squeezed between purchase prices from distributors and retail selling prices to consumers. Without the right purchasing strategy, potential profits can erode. Key challenges include:

*   **Price Fluctuations**: Prices of basic commodities like rice, cooking oil, and sugar can change rapidly, making stock planning and pricing difficult.
*   **Limited Capital**: Warungs typically have limited capital, often making large-scale purchases to secure lower prices unfeasible.
*   **Competition**: A highly competitive environment demands warungs to offer attractive prices without sacrificing profits.

### 1. Tiered Pricing Strategy

The tiered pricing concept allows warungs to obtain lower prices as their purchase volume increases. This is highly beneficial for warungs that make large wholesale purchases, such as sacked rice or cartoned cooking oil.

**How Tiered Pricing Works:**

*   **Small Volume**: Standard price for single units or small quantities.
*   **Medium Volume**: Special lower unit price for medium-sized purchases (e.g., 3-5 sacks of rice, 5-10 cartons of cooking oil).
*   **Large Volume**: The most optimal wholesale price for large-volume purchases (e.g., >5 sacks of rice, >10 cartons of cooking oil).

This strategy directly helps warungs maintain profit margins because the cost per unit becomes more efficient. For instance, a warung purchasing 10 sacks of C4/Rojolele rice will get a significantly cheaper price per sack compared to buying only 1 or 2 sacks.

### 2. Staple Food Bundling Promotions
