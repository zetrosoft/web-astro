## Zero Food Waste Preservation Guide

Ingredient decay prior to cooking is the leading cause of inflated kitchen budgets. Mastering fundamental food preservation techniques ensures weekly grocery purchases achieve near 100% utilization.

Different food categories require distinct microclimate management inside the refrigerator. Disciplined food reserve handling forms an essential safeguard in protecting domestic financial resilience against economic volatility.

| Food Category | Pre-Storage Preparation Method | Container & Fridge Location | Max Shelf Life |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Leafy Greens** | Do not wash; wrap in dry paper towels | Chiller / Airtight Container | 7 - 10 Days |
| **Tempeh** | Portion out; steam until medium-cooked | Sealed Container in Chiller | 7 - 12 Days |
| **Tofu** | Wash clean; submerge in clean water (change every 2 days) | Main Chiller Shelf (Not Freezer) | 7 - 10 Days |
| **Chicken Eggs** | Store on middle shelf (not door); do not wash | Original Carton / Egg Tray | 3 - 4 Weeks |
| **Steamed Carbs** | Cool completely before sealing | Individual Portions in Freezer | 1 Month |

## Tactical Kitchen Execution: 90-Minute Batch Cooking Strategy

Executing meal prep does not require sacrificing your entire weekend. An efficient batch cooking workflow divides the process into three parallel stages:

1. **Ingredient Prep (20 Mins)**: Dice tempeh and tofu into daily portion sizes. Marinate half with garlic, coriander, and salt, leaving the remainder unseasoned for versatile stir-fries.
2. **Parallel Steaming & Baking (40 Mins)**: Use a multi-tier steamer to cook tempeh and hard-boil eggs simultaneously. Concurrently, bake or air-fry tofu to eliminate excess oil usage.
3. **Cooling & Portioning (30 Mins)**: Transfer cooked and prepped items into separate BPA-free containers matching the daily plan. Label each container with prep dates.

Combining rigorous cost-per-gram protein calculations, zero-waste preservation protocols, and a structured cooking routine turns kitchen efficiency into measurable savings. Families can enjoy nutrient-dense 2,000-calorie daily meals while maintaining strong fiscal health throughout 2026.
