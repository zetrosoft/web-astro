ERPNext v16 changes the game with its **100% open-source GNU GPL v3 license**. No per-user fees, no hidden costs, and total freedom to use, modify, and distribute the software. Businesses can reallocate license budgets to strategic investments: product development, market expansion, or HR. ## Optimizing Supply Chain and Production with Advanced ERPNext v16 Modules

ERPNext v16 significantly enhances its core modules, tailored for complex manufacturing and distribution needs. This isn't just an upgrade; it's a leap in capabilities, focused on operational efficiency and end-to-end data visibility:

### Smarter Accounting Module

*   **Enhanced Financial Reporting:** More detailed and customizable financial reports, enabling in-depth profitability analysis for each product line or distribution branch. For SMEs like corner shops, this means simple yet accurate bookkeeping, separating wholesale vs. retail revenue, and better tracking of working capital.
*   **Journal Automation:** Automated accounting journal features for recurring transactions, reducing manual errors and accelerating monthly closing processes.

### Adaptive Stock Management

*   **Stock Reservation:** A crucial new feature for distribution and manufacturing businesses. You can allocate stock for specific sales orders, ensuring item availability and preventing overselling, even if physical stock has not yet been received. This is vital for high-turnover commodities like eggs or flour.
*   **Multi-Warehouse & Multi-Location:** Better inventory management across multiple warehouses or storage locations, providing real-time stock visibility throughout the entire supply chain.
*   **Batch & Serial Number Tracking:** Item tracking based on batch or serial numbers for more effective quality control and product recalls, especially for food products or electronics.

### Comprehensive Manufacturing Module

*   **Improved Material Requirements Planning (MRP):** More accurate material requirements planning, considering supplier lead times and production capacity, minimizing overstock or out-of-stock situations.
*   **Inward Subcontracting:** Full support for subcontracting processes where raw materials are handed over to third-party vendors for processing and returned as finished or semi-finished goods. This optimizes complex manufacturing supply chains.
*   **Work Order & Production Chain Management:** More intuitive work order management, allowing for more efficient tracking of production status from raw materials to final products.
