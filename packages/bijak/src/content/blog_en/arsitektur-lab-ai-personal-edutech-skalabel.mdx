### Foundations of a Modular System Architecture: Event-Driven vs. Monolith

When building a personal-scale artificial intelligence lab, a common architectural pitfall is relying on monolithic system designs. When data scrapers, text preprocessing modules, vector storage libraries, and user interfaces share a single runtime dependency, a failure in one module collapses the entire experiment pipeline.

A far more resilient approach is breaking the system down into an event-driven architecture. By decoupling components into containerized microservices, every process—such as document ingestion, embedding extraction, and model inference—runs within strictly controlled resource boundaries.

### Database Scalability Strategies for Self-Hosted AI Data

The volume of data required for training or fine-tuning AI models grows exponentially. Within a self-hosted AI lab ecosystem, handling context-based search systems like Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) demands unprecedented database query performance. Traditional relational databases without specialized optimization quickly hit severe latency bottlenecks when processing high-dimensional vector searches.

To maintain query latency below tens of milliseconds, choosing proper data indexing strategies and storage architectures is essential. Implementing database sharding, setting up read replicas to isolate heavy research queries, and leveraging vector search extensions like pgvector represent industry standards for modern AI architectures.

Technical insights on [optimasi pgvector untuk AI](/en/blog/pgvector-optimasi-skala-ai-produksi-hostinger-kvm-vps/) demonstrate that configuring HNSW (Hierarchical Navigable Small World) indexes properly can boost vector similarity search speeds up to 10 times compared to default sequential scans.

| **Monolith (Single Node Postgres)** | Very Low | High (on datasets > 1M vectors) | Very High | Initial experiments & small-scale RAG prototyping |
| **Read Replicas + HNSW Indexing** | Moderate | Very Low | High | Simultaneous AI dataset reads & continuous pipelines |
| **Database Sharding (Distributed)** | High | Low to Moderate | Moderate | Multi-region big data processing & research repos |
| **Hybrid Vector-Relational Cache** | Moderate | Fastest (In-Memory Latency) | High | Interactive EduTech apps demanding real-time responses |
