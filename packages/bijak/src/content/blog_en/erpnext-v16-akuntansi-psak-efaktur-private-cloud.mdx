## ERPNext Accounting & PSAK: E-Invoice Integration and Data Sovereignty for Indonesian Corporations

Indonesian enterprise companies frequently face significant challenges with their accounting systems. PT. Bhakti Jaya Abadi, a national manufacturing and distribution group with dozens of branches, experienced these firsthand. Their legacy ERP, though functional, burdened operations with escalating per-user license costs, rigid accounting processes slow to adapt to current PSAK standards, and semi-automated, error-prone e-Faktur integration.

Chief Financial Officer, Ms. Anita, often voiced concerns about the company's sensitive financial data stored in foreign public clouds. “Data sovereignty is a top priority,” she emphasized in a strategic meeting. “We need a solution that is not only efficient and regulatory compliant but also provides full control over our crucial information.” Meanwhile, Head of IT Department, Mr. Budi, continuously sought alternatives to cut down the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) without sacrificing functionality and security.

## Accounting Transformation with ERPNext v16 and Frappe Framework

PT. Bhakti Jaya Abadi's search ultimately led them to ERPNext v16, an open-source ERP system built on the Frappe Framework, offering flexibility and efficiency. Initially, skepticism arose, as open-source solutions are often perceived as less mature for enterprise scale. However, after in-depth research and reviewing case studies of implementations in similar corporations, PT. Bhakti Jaya Abadi's IT and finance teams began to realize its significant potential.

The Accounting module in ERPNext v16 is highly adaptable. The team can now configure chart of accounts, analytical dimensions, and financial reports to fully [comply with PSAK standards](/blog/erpnext-accounting-psak/). The Frappe Framework as its foundation allows for deep customization without modifying core code—a significant advantage over rigid proprietary systems. Its modular, Python-based architecture also facilitates seamless integration with other systems. ### Automated E-Invoice Integration: Efficiency Without Compromise

One of the critical points for PT. Bhakti Jaya Abadi was the automation of the e-Faktur process. Previously, this process involved many manual steps, from exporting data from the ERP, importing it into the DJP e-Faktur application, to validation and upload. This was not only time-consuming but also prone to human error.

With the assistance of implementation partner Samkarsa.com, PT. Bhakti Jaya Abadi successfully integrated e-Faktur efficiently within the ERPNext ecosystem. This integration allows sales and purchase invoice data to be automatically retrieved, processed, and submitted to the DJP via secure APIs, minimizing manual intervention. As a result, tax reporting processes are faster, more accurate, and fully compliant with Indonesian tax regulations.
