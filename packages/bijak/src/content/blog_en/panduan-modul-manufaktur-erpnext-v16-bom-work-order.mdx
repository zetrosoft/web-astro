### Flexible Multi-Level Bill of Materials (BOM) and Workstation Routing Architecture

The Bill of Materials (BOM) is central to any manufacturing process. ERPNext v16, built on the robust Frappe Framework, makes it easy to define complex multi-level BOM architectures. This means you can manage raw materials, components, and sub-assemblies needed for a finished product, even for items with intricate layered structures. Each BOM item can have its own *routing*, defining the sequence of workstations or operations it must pass through. This ensures every production stage is well-planned, from raw material preparation to final assembly, minimizing scrap and accelerating throughput.

This flexibility also includes managing BOM versions, allowing factories to adapt to product design changes or raw material availability without disrupting operations. With full visibility at every BOM level, production managers can identify potential bottlenecks and proactively optimize workflows.

### Automated Work Order Cycle, Shop Floor Job Cards, and Machine Capacity Allocation

From planning to execution on the factory floor, ERPNext v16 automates the entire Work Order cycle. Once a Sales Order is received or a Production Plan is created, the system automatically generates Work Orders detailing the product, quantity, and due date. These Work Orders are then broken down into individual Job Cards for shop floor operators, providing clear instructions on tasks, raw materials needed, and workstations to use.

The system also smartly manages machine and labor capacity allocation. Using *real-time* data on machine availability and maintenance schedules, ERPNext v16 helps prevent *overload* or *idle time*, ensuring optimal resource utilization. Digital tracking of Job Card progress enables managers to monitor production status in *real-time*, identify delays, and take swift corrective actions, removing the need for error-prone manual tracking.

### Automated Cost Accounting Journal Mapping: From Raw Material to Finished Goods

A key advantage of ERPNext v16 is its ability to automate perpetual cost accounting entries, aligning with international accounting standards. This eliminates time-consuming and error-prone manual journal entries, especially for COGS calculation. Every movement of raw materials, work-in-progress (WIP), and finished goods automatically triggers the correct journal entry, offering an accurate and up-to-date financial picture.

Here's a table detailing the automatic debit/credit flow:

| Document Stage | Debit Account | Credit Account | Accounting Impact & Standard |
| :-------------- | :------------ | :------------- | :--------------------------- |
| **1. Raw Material Issue (Stock Entry - Material Issue)** | Work In Progress (WIP) | Raw Material Inventory | Reduces raw material assets, charges to WIP. Compliant with relevant inventory accounting standards. |
| **2. Direct Labor Cost Recording (Journal Entry/Payroll)** | Work In Progress (WIP) | Wages Payable/Cash | Adds direct labor costs to WIP. |
| **3. Factory Overhead Allocation (Journal Entry/Cost Allocation)** | Work In Progress (WIP) | Accumulated Factory Overhead Applied | Adds factory overhead costs to WIP. |
| **4. Finished Goods Completion (Stock Entry - Manufacture/Finish)** | Finished Goods Inventory | Work In Progress (WIP) | Reduces WIP, increases finished goods assets. Records Cost of Goods Manufactured (COGM). Compliant with inventory accounting standards. |
| **5. Finished Goods Sale (Sales Invoice/Delivery Note)** | Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | Finished Goods Inventory | Reduces sold finished goods assets, records COGS. Compliant with inventory and revenue recognition standards. |

### Subcontracting Management Without Losing Track of Raw Material Balances

For factories using subcontracting services or external processing, ERPNext v16 provides comprehensive features. Whether it's *inward subcontracting* (receiving materials from a customer for processing) or *outward subcontracting* (sending materials to a vendor for processing), the system accurately tracks raw material and semi-finished goods balances. You can easily create Purchase Orders for subcontracting, issue raw materials to vendors, and receive back finished goods, all clearly recorded within the system. This prevents inventory from being lost and ensures full accountability with third parties.
