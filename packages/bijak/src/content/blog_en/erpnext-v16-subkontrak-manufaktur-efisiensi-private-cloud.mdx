A precision parts manufacturer recently discovered a Rp 450 million inventory gap. The stock simply 'vanished' between the main plant and three subcontracting partners. This is a common crisis. In manufacturing outsourcing, failing to trace raw stock sent to third parties is the fastest way to lose control of production costs. Without tight workflow integration, cash leakage becomes inevitable.

### Anatomy of Subcontracting Workflows in ERPNext v16

Fixing this starts with [mastering core manufacturing modules](/en/blog/panduan-modul-manufaktur-erpnext-v16-bom-work-order/) like BOMs and Work Orders. ERPNext v16 automates the subcontracting lifecycle through four integrated stages:

1. **Subcontracted Purchase Order (PO):** The system flags subcontracting needs and binds the specific BOM containing client-owned raw stock and vendor service fees.
2. **Material Issue & Stock Transfer:** Dispatching raw materials to a supplier warehouse updates locations dynamically without prematurely derecognizing asset values.
3. **Subcontracting Receipt:** Upon receiving finished items, ERPNext v16 automatically merges vendor service charges into the item's valuation.
4. **Automated Scrap & Loss:** Accurately tracks material scrap tolerances to prevent unmapped inventory loss at vendor sites.

**1. How is material loss handled?**
ERPNext v16 uses an *Allowed Loss Percentage* in the BOM. Any variance exceeding this is flagged as an operational loss and can be billed back to the vendor.
