## Optimizing Inventory Control and Production Scheduling

Within the manufacturing landscape, inventory management extends far beyond simply logging incoming and outgoing goods. The First Expired, First Out (FEFO) methodology paired with end-to-end serial number tracking ensures zero material wastage caused by human error.

Furthermore, enhancements in Material Requirements Planning (MRP) and Finite Capacity Scheduling (FCS) empower planning teams to map real machine capacities accurately. The system automatically triggers alerts whenever work scheduling allocations exceed actual factory capacities, guaranteeing adherence to client delivery deadlines without compromising product quality.
